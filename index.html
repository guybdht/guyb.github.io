Edgecase Detector

A tool for FPGA verification: pick the kind of hardware block you're building, and it surfaces the edge cases its testbench should cover.

  • The core: a database that maps block types (FIFOs, arbiters, FSMs, interfaces) to the edge cases that break them.
  • Data: mined from open RTL repositories with their testbenches, and from published silicon errata.
  • Measured by mutation testing: inject bugs into the RTL and count how many the suggested tests catch.
  • Why FPGA: most verification tooling is built for ASIC teams; FPGA teams are underserved.
